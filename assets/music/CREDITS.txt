Picnic Pals music credits
=========================

The background tunes and the win jingle are original music written for Picnic Pals.

Recorded instruments come from the FreePats project and are dedicated to the
public domain (CC0 1.0). Banks used: Upright Piano KW, Spanish Classical Guitar,
Finger Bass YR, Concert Harp, Kalimba, Ukulele, World Percussion, and Xylophone
Medium Mallets. Authors and performers include Gonzalo and Roberto of FreePats,
Inma Martínez de Miguel, Andrea Biasior, Xavimart (Javier), Mateusz Dąbrowski,
and the Versilian Community Sample Library (Versilian Studios; Concert Harp
performed by K. Smith). https://freepats.zenvoid.org/

The flute is an original synthesized instrument written for Picnic Pals, not a
third-party sample.

Soundfont (.sf2) files are not shipped with the game.
